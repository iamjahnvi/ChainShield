STEP 2 OF 4

Look beyond the surface.

Websites can look convincing. ChainShield combines independent checks to help you investigate suspicious signals and understand where the evidence comes from.

  • 01
    TinyFish Web Intelligence
    Retrieves relevant public search evidence about the investigated hostname. Search relevance is context, not proof of malicious activity.
  • 02
    Threat Reputation
    Checks the submitted URL using Google Safe Browsing.
  • 03
    URL Structure
    Examines suspicious URL patterns such as IP hostnames, punycode labels, and unusually deep subdomains.
  • 04
    Ethereum Intelligence
    When a contract address is provided, retrieves on-chain observations and analyzes verified source metadata and proxy implementations.
Investigation flow: web + optional Web3
STEP 3 OF 4

Look deeper into Web3.

When a website involves a smart contract, ChainShield can inspect selected on-chain details and available verified source code to surface capabilities that deserve closer review.

  • □
    Contract presence
    Checks whether bytecode is returned for the supplied address at the selected block reference. Bytecode presence or absence does not establish whether an address is safe or malicious, or prove that it is an externally owned account.
  • □
    Privileged capabilities
    Looks for source-code patterns associated with minting, pausing, blacklist controls, upgradeability, and privileged administration. Detected indicators do not establish malicious behavior.
  • □
    Proxy implementations
    Follows validated implementation metadata through a bounded proxy chain to reduce the chance of overlooking relevant implementation source.
  • □
    Evidence and uncertainty
    Distinguishes completed checks from incomplete or unavailable analysis. No detected indicators do not establish that a contract is safe.
